PHASE 6 - STAGE E: RUNBOOK (one page per system)
================================================================

Use ../phase7/runbook.md as the template. A runbook is good when
someone else could deploy, roll back and debug using only that page.

MUST ANSWER:
  1. What does it do, and who uses it?
  2. How do I deploy it? (exact commands)
  3. How do I roll back? (keep the previous image tag; see runbook)
  4. What do I check when it breaks? (health, logs, provider status, budget)
  5. What does it cost per month? (API + VPS)
  6. Who do I call?

ROLLBACK TIP: tag every deploy (git tag v1.0.3), keep the previous
  image, and rehearse the rollback once on a quiet day.

PHASE 6 EXIT CHECKLIST
  [ ] One app live on a VPS over HTTPS
  [ ] Push to main deploys after tests pass
  [ ] No secret in the repo or the image
  [ ] Retries + fallback proven by killing the provider on purpose
  [ ] Backup restore tested once
  [ ] Runbook written

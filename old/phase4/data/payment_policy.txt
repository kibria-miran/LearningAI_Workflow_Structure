Payment Policy

We accept the following payment methods:
- Credit cards: Visa, MasterCard, American Express
- PayPal: Link your PayPal account for secure payments
- Bank transfer: For orders over $500
- Installments: Available for orders over $200 via Klarna

Payment security:
- All payment information is encrypted using TLS 1.2+
- We do not store full credit card numbers
- PCI-DSS compliant payment processing
- Fraud detection tools monitor all transactions

Payment issues:
- declined cards: Contact your bank or try an alternative payment method
- failed transactions: Retry after 15 minutes or use alternative method
- installment plans: First payment due immediately, remaining spread over 3 months

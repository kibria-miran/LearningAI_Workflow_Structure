Refund Policy Documentation

Our refund policy allows customers to return products within 30 days of purchase if the items are in their original condition.

Key points:
- 30-day return window from the date of purchase
- Items must be in original, unopened condition
- Proof of purchase (receipt or order number) is required
- Refunds are processed to the original payment method
- Standard refund processing time is 5-7 business days after receipt
- Shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error
- Damaged or defective items can be returned within 90 days for replacement or refund

Contact customer support for exceptions or special circumstances.

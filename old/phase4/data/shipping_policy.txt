Shipping Policy

All orders are shipped within 1-2 business days from our warehouse.

Shipping options:
- Standard shipping: 2-5 business days, $5.99 flat rate
- Express shipping: 1-2 business days, $14.99 flat rate
- Free shipping: Orders over $99 ship free

International shipping:
- Available to most countries
- Delivery times vary by location (7-21 business days)
- Customs duties and taxes are the customer's responsibility

Tracking:
- All shipments include tracking number
- Tracking updates provided via email
- Real-time tracking available on carrier website

Returns:
- Unwanted items can be returned within 30 days
- Return shipping label provided via email
- Refund processed within 5-7 business days after return received
